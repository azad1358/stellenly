How the Hiring Score works

It rates how NetBird hires — based only on its current job postings on Stellenly, not on employee reviews. Each factor gives 0–100 points; the total is their weighted average (100 points = 5 stars):

  • Visa & relocation — 15%

  • Stable postings — 15%
