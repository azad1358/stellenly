      About Stellenly

      This site is free, ad-light and sets no cookies

      We respect your privacy. Your job search is your business — and keeping your personal data private is your right.

      • No cookies and no ad trackers
      • No accounts, no profiles, no profiling
      • No CV uploads — nothing about you is stored on our side
      • Saved jobs stay in your browser, never on our servers
      • Anonymous visit counts only, to see which pages help
