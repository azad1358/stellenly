Local storage in your browser

We use your browser's localStorage only for features you actively use (saved jobs, applied list, hidden companies, theme). This is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The data never leaves your device.

Support / donations

The “Support” button links to Ko-fi. If you choose to pay, Ko-fi and its payment providers process your data under their own privacy policy. We receive only what Ko-fi shows us (e.g. a name you choose to display).

Partner (affiliate) links and sponsor

Some links on the Resources page and in guides are partner links, marked “Partner link”. This site doesn't set any cookie or tracker for them. If you click one, the partner (or its affiliate network) may set its own cookie on its site to recognise that you came from us, and may pay us a commission if you sign up. That happens under the partner's own privacy policy. The sponsor box is a plain link, with no tracking.

External links

Job postings link to the original employer or job board. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site.
