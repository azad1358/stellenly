Job database

Job searches are sent to our database hosted by Supabase in the EU region [Frankfurt]. A search contains only your search terms and filters (including companies and words you chose to hide); it contains no name, account or other personal data, and we don't store search requests. Supabase processes your IP address to deliver the response (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). [Link to Supabase DPA.]

“I got hired” counter

If you click “Yes, I got hired!”, we store only the time of the click to update a public counter. Nothing else is stored.

Local storage in your browser

We use your browser's localStorage only for features you actively use (saved jobs, applied list, hidden companies, theme). This is strictly necessary to provide the function you requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The data never leaves your device.

Support / donations

The “Support” button links to Ko-fi. If you choose to pay, Ko-fi and its payment providers process your data under their own privacy policy. We receive only what Ko-fi shows us (e.g. a name you choose to display).

Partner (affiliate) links and sponsor

Some links on the Resources page and in guides are partner links, marked “Partner link”. This site doesn't set any cookie or tracker for them. If you click one, the partner (or its affiliate network) may set its own cookie on its site to recognise that you came from us, and may pay us a commission if you sign up. That happens under the partner's own privacy policy. The sponsor box is a plain link, with no tracking.

Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To see which pages are useful, we count visits with GoatCounter (goatcounter.com, an open-source analytics service). It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device. For each page view it receives the page address, the referring site, your browser's screen size and language, and your IP address and user agent. GoatCounter uses the IP address and user agent only briefly to tell unique visits apart and does not keep them; we only ever see anonymous totals. [Check GoatCounter's privacy policy for where its servers are located and add a data processing agreement if required.] We also count a few anonymous actions — for example that an “Apply” link or a job was opened, or which words were searched for — without any information about who did it. Please don't type personal data into the search box. To show which jobs are popular, we also count per job and day how often it was opened and how often “Apply” was clicked — just these totals, with nothing about the visitor (no IP address, no identifier, no cookie). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the site). If you use an ad or tracking blocker, it may block GoatCounter; the site works the same. GoatCounter privacy policy.

Company map

The map on the Companies page is off until you click “Show map”. Only then does your browser load map images (tiles) from the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cambridge, UK; privacy policy), which receives your IP address to deliver them (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, consent). If you tick “Remember my choice”, that choice is stored only in your browser's localStorage. The map code itself is hosted on this site. City coordinates are looked up in advance on our side (OpenStreetMap Nominatim) and contain no visitor data.

External links

Job postings link to the original employer or job board. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site.
