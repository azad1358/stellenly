The EU Blue Card is the residence permit most non-EU professionals aim for when they move to Germany for a job. The most common reason applications get delayed or refused is simple: **the salary in the contract is below the threshold.** Here is how to check an offer in two minutes.

## The 2026 thresholds

| Who | Minimum gross salary per year (2026) |
|---|---|
| Standard (most jobs) | **€50,700** (about €4,225 per month) |
| Shortage occupations, recent graduates, IT specialists without a degree | **€45,934.20** (about €3,828 per month) |

The thresholds are tied to Germany's pension insurance contribution ceiling, so **they go up every January**. An offer that was fine last year may not be enough this year.

The lower threshold applies to so-called shortage occupations, which include IT, engineering, natural sciences, mathematics and healthcare roles. It also applies to people who graduated within the last three years and to IT specialists with professional experience but no university degree. For the lower threshold, the Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur für Arbeit) usually has to approve the job, which can add a few weeks.

## What counts as "salary"

- **Gross, fixed and guaranteed.** Your base salary in the contract is what counts.
- **Bonuses usually don't count** if they are variable or not guaranteed. A €48,000 base salary plus a "likely" €5,000 bonus is assessed as €48,000.
- **Yearly, not monthly.** German offers are almost always quoted as gross per year. If you only see a monthly figure, multiply by 12 (and check whether a 13th salary is contractually guaranteed).

## Quick check for any job you find

1. Look at the **lowest** number of the salary range. Only that is safe.
2. Compare it with the right threshold above.
3. If the job shows no salary, ask early in the process. It's a normal question in Germany.

## Beyond salary

The salary is only one condition. You also typically need:

- a recognised university degree (or, for IT specialists, relevant professional experience instead),
- a concrete job offer or signed contract that matches your qualification,
- the usual documents: passport, contract, proof of qualification, health insurance.

## Bottom line

Check the **guaranteed base salary** in your contract against the **current year's** threshold, and leave some headroom: thresholds rise every January, and that matters if your start date slips into the next year.
