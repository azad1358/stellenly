German job offers always quote **gross salary per year**. What reaches your bank account each month is quite a bit lower. Right now **{{stat:salary_pct}}%** of the jobs on this site show a salary — here is how to read it.

## What gets deducted

| Deduction | Who pays | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Income tax (*Lohnsteuer*) | you | progressive: 0% up to the basic allowance, rising to 42% (45% for very high incomes) |
| Pension insurance | split with employer | about 9% of gross for you |
| Health insurance | split with employer | about 8–9% for you, depending on your insurer |
| Long-term care insurance | split with employer | about 1.8% for you; a surcharge applies if you are over 23 and have no children |
| Unemployment insurance | split with employer | about 1.3% for you |
| Church tax | only church members | 8–9% **of your income tax** |

Social security together takes **around 20–21% of gross** from you, up to yearly contribution ceilings. Above the ceilings the percentage falls, which is why net pay rises faster for high salaries.

## Tax classes (Steuerklassen)

- **Class I** — single. This is where most people start.
- **Class III / V** — married couples with very different incomes can split this way.
- **Class IV** — married couples with similar incomes.
- **Class VI** — a second job.

Your tax class only changes the monthly withholding. The final tax is settled when you file your yearly tax return, and many employees get money back.

## A rule of thumb

For a single person in tax class I, not a church member, with a salary between €50,000 and €70,000, **net pay is usually around 60–65% of gross**. For a real number, use the official calculator from the Federal Ministry of Finance (link below) — it takes two minutes.

## Comparing two offers

1. Compare **net**, not gross, if the offers are in different countries.
2. Count everything: 13th salary, bonus, pension contribution, public transport ticket, meal vouchers.
3. Remember the **Blue Card thresholds** apply to gross salary — see [EU Blue Card 2026](eu-blue-card-salary-2026.html).

## Don't register as a church member by accident

When you register your address, the form asks for your religion. If you write a church that collects church tax (Catholic, Protestant and a few others), you will pay it. Leave it empty if you aren't a member.
